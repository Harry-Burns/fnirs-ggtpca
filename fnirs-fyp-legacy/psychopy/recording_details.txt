RECORDING DETAILS


Environment - 
    The recording will take place in a special room in the "Centre for Human Brain Health" on the UOB campus. This is where the LUMO headset is stored. 
    The sujects will be sat down in a chair, and then I will fit the LUMO headset onto their heads. 
    This will be repeated until the LUMO application indicates that the headset is coupled correctly.
    Next I will automatically adjust the saturation of the optodes in the headset using the default method in LUMO.

    The subject will be sat in front of a monitor that is giving them instructions, and they will be hands-free throughout the test.
    


Optode Placement -
    - Optodes will be placed on a single side of the head, around the motor cortex region that is on the opposite hemisphere to the hand that is tapping.
        - For this experiment, I have chosen the right hand (and therefore left hemisphere) as the majority of my subjects will be right-handed, so the activation signal should be stronger.
        - There will be 7 optodes placed around this area in a hexagonal pattern.



Each recording will cover 3 individual trials, each with a different type. These types will be 
    NHM - No Motion,
    SHM - Eyebrow/face movements, 
    LHM - Entire head movements (nodding/shaking)

The trials will be identical, other than the fact that motion will be purposely induced by the subject in types 2 and 3.
Each trial will consist of 8 activation-rest blocks, and a starting & ending baseline.

Timings - 
    3 Trials per recording: 8 blocks + 2 baselines per trial.

    Action time: 10s
    Rest time: 20s
    Baseline time: 20s

    8 * (10 + 20) + 2 * 20 = 280s per trial
    280 * 3 = 840s recording = 14mins

Task - Motor Activation
    Finger tapping with 1 hand, to localize the activation to just 1 side of the hemisphere.
    There will only be 1s of motion in each motion infected task




Dont do coutdown to task, but can do countdown to motion
